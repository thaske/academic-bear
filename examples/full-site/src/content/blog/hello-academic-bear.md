This is a complete example post rendered with the shared `BlogPost` layout from
Academic Bear.

The consuming site owns its content collection, routes, homepage copy, and site
configuration. Academic Bear provides the reusable presentation layer.

## What is included?

- A home page
- A publication list
- A blog index with tag links
- Individual blog post pages
- RSS and sitemap support
